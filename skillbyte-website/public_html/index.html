How we work

Four stages, no black box

You will know what is happening at every point, because you are in it. No monthly report that arrives three weeks late explaining what we already spent.

  1. 01

    Listen

    A conversation about your numbers and what is actually broken. Free, and often it ends with us saying you do not need us yet.

  2. 02

    Plan

    A written scope with fixed pricing, a timeline, and the specific metric we are trying to move. You approve it before anyone starts.

  3. 03

    Build

    Weekly demos of real work, not status decks. You see it as it comes together and can change direction while it is still cheap.

  4. 04

    Grow

    Launch is the start. We measure against the metric from stage two and keep tuning — or hand you the keys and train your team.
