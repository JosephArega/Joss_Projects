Habesha Kitchen

How a checkout rebuild more than tripled online orders for a four-branch restaurant group.

Client

Habesha Kitchen

Services

Web development, SEO

Timeline

7 weeks

Result

+214% online orders

The problem

Four branches, a growing delivery business, and a checkout that took eleven taps to complete. Roughly two thirds of the orders that started never finished. The owner assumed the issue was demand, and was about to spend on advertising to fix it.

What we found

Most traffic arrived from a Telegram channel, opening inside Telegram’s in-app browser — where the payment step silently failed. Nobody had tested that path, because nobody on the team browsed that way. Advertising into that funnel would have burned the budget and taught them nothing.

What we did

Rebuilt the ordering flow down to three steps, made it work in every in-app browser we could get our hands on, and added the analytics needed to see where people were dropping out. Then local SEO, so the branches actually surfaced in map searches.

Results after six months

+214%Online orders
3Steps to checkout, from 11
+81%Map search impressions
0Birr spent on ads
