    About us

    A small team that would rather do fewer things well.

    SkillByte is a digital consultancy in Addis Ababa. We build marketing, mobile apps and websites for businesses across Ethiopia and East Africa — and we stay involved long enough to find out whether the work actually did anything.

    Our story

    We started because too much good money was being wasted

    SkillByte started in 2017 with two people and one conviction: that most of what businesses here were being sold as “digital” was not built to work.

    We kept meeting business owners who had paid for a website they could not update, an app that never launched, or a marketing retainer that produced a monthly PDF and nothing else. Not because anyone set out to cheat them — mostly because the people selling the work were not the people doing it, and nobody in the chain was accountable for whether it worked.

    So we built the opposite. A small team where the people you meet are the people who do the work. Fixed prices agreed before anyone starts. A named metric for every project. And a standing offer to train your team to take the work in-house, because a client who stays by choice is worth more than one who stays because they cannot leave.

    We are not the cheapest option in Addis, and we are not trying to be the biggest. We would rather have twenty clients who would recommend us without being asked.

    What we believe

    • The people you meet should be the people who do the work
    • A fixed price beats an hourly estimate that drifts
    • Every project needs one number it is trying to move
    • You own your code, accounts and data — always
    • Saying “you do not need this” is part of the job

    Where we work

    Based on Bole Road, Addis Ababa. We work with clients across Ethiopia, Kenya, Uganda and Rwanda. Most delivery is remote; for bigger engagements we travel for kickoff and the key milestones, because some conversations are worth having in a room.

    Working hours are Monday to Friday, 8:30 to 17:30 EAT. We answer enquiries within one business day.

    How we think

    Three habits that shape the work

    1

    Start with the number

    Before design or code, we agree what this project is supposed to move — orders, enquiries, support tickets, churn. It settles most arguments later, because there is a shared definition of done that is not a matter of taste.

    2

    Build for the worst connection

    We test on mid-range Android over throttled mobile data, not on a fast laptop. Designing for the hardest conditions your users face is the single cheapest way to improve results across the board.

    3

    Hand over the keys

    Documentation, training and accounts in your name, from the start. If you want to run it yourself, that is a successful outcome rather than a lost account.
